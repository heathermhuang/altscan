# Licensing

Altscan is **open source under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE)** — an OSI-approved licence. A
separate commercial licence is available for organisations that cannot use AGPL code.

## What you can do under AGPL-3.0, for free, forever

No permission needed, no fee, no attribution banner, no audit clause:

- **Run it.** Deploy Altscan for any purpose, including commercially and as a paid service.
- **Fork it.** Modify anything, for any reason.
- **Distribute it.** Share your fork publicly or privately.
- **Sell services around it.** Hosting, support, consulting, managed deployments, RaaS.
- **Keep your changes.** You own your modifications.

The licence grant is **irrevocable**. Once a release is published under AGPL-3.0, that
release stays AGPL-3.0. We cannot take it back, and neither can anyone who acquires us.

## What AGPL-3.0 asks in return

**One thing:** if you modify Altscan and let others use it over a network, you must offer
those users the source of your modified version, under AGPL-3.0.

That's it. If you run Altscan unmodified, you owe nothing. If your modifications are
purely internal and no external user touches them, you owe nothing.

## When you need a commercial licence

AGPL works for almost everyone. You need a commercial licence in one case: **you want to
build on Altscan without releasing your modifications.**

In practice that means:

- Your organisation has a policy prohibiting AGPL software in its stack.
- You are embedding Altscan into a proprietary product you cannot open-source.
- You need warranty, indemnity, or a support SLA that AGPL explicitly disclaims.

Commercial licensing removes the source-disclosure obligation and is negotiated per
deployment. To enquire, open a GitHub discussion or use the contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## How this compares

Dual licensing is common in this space; the terms of the free tier vary a great deal.
The distinction that matters is what the *non-paying* user is permitted to do.

| | Altscan (AGPL-3.0) | Typical source-available licence |
|---|---|---|
| OSI-approved open source | Yes | No |
| Grant is irrevocable | Yes | Often revocable at the licensor's discretion |
| Licensor can change terms unilaterally | No | Often yes, with continued use deemed acceptance |
| Run commercially / charge for hosting | Yes | Usually requires a paid commercial licence |
| Distribute a modified version | Yes, under AGPL-3.0 | Often prohibited without a paid licence |
| Mandatory vendor branding in your UI | No | Sometimes required |
| Licensor claims rights over your modifications | No | Sometimes yes |
| Compliance audit rights over your business | No | Sometimes yes |

We are describing a category, not a specific competitor — check the actual licence text
of anything you are evaluating, including ours. Licence terms change; the right-hand
column reflects patterns we have seen, not any single product.
