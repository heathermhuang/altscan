- **Malformed page numbers now open page 1 on every list page.** A `?page=` value that is not a plain number, such as `1e2` or `3abc`, used to open a different page on the address and token pages than elsewhere. For self-hosters, a malformed `BODY_CACHE_TTL_MS` or `BODY_CACHE_WINDOW_BYTES` now falls back to its default instead of a near-zero or broken cache setting (#358).
